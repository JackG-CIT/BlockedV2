Sound test

Every sound in the game is made by static/js/audio.js. Click to listen, then change the numbers or notes there and refresh.

Music

Click a track to listen. Switching tracks waits for the bar line and crossfades, the same as between pages in the game. The gold tags show which slot each track fills; change that in PLAYLIST near the top of the music section of audio.js.

The long chapter tracks play their short loop over and over, and every few times round they play a variation instead: a change-up (a second fiddle and drum fills), a grand pass (horns on the tune, big drums) or a breakdown (just fiddle, plucks and light drums). The cycle line shows the order. The variation buttons jump straight to that pass so you don't have to wait. Each chapter's boss track is a faster, grander version of its tune.
